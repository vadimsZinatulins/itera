# 13: Correct, remove and reset reviewed understanding

**What to build:** Let the learner correct or remove reviewed observations directly from Explore understanding, or reset the Subject's understanding without deleting its study material or conversations.

**Blocked by:** 12 — Explore Subject understanding through connected concepts.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Correct an observation and explicitly save the correction; the map/details and future practice context reflect the new reviewed text without changing documents or conversation history.
- [ ] Remove an observation so it is absent from future learning interpretation and practice context, not merely hidden visually.
- [ ] Provide a clearly scoped, confirmed reset of Subject understanding. Preserve Learning Documents and conversations, and explain that the record of understanding is what changes.
- [ ] Existing conversations do not automatically rebuild deliberately removed/reset observations. Newly reviewed recaps may contribute again only through fresh review rather than replay/resurrection of old state.
- [ ] Enforce understanding-revision preconditions for corrections, removals, reset and recap application. An old tab/proposal cannot undo a newer reset or silently overwrite a correction; retain attempted text and explain refresh/review.
- [ ] Delayed provider results and repeated acceptance requests cannot recreate removed reviewed state or apply a pre-reset proposal without current review.
- [ ] All actions are account/Subject scoped and persist across devices. Existing qualification/uncertainty remains visible; corrections do not imply calibrated mastery.
- [ ] Public-boundary tests cover correction/removal effects on outgoing practice context, reset with retained documents/history, no automatic reconstruction, repeated operations, two-session stale applications and foreign-account denial.
