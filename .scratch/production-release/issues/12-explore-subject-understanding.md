# 12: Explore Subject understanding through connected concepts

**What to build:** Let the learner open Explore understanding from the Learning Trail, inspect related concepts and distinguish tentative conversation activity from reviewed observations and unexplored material.

**Blocked by:** 09 — Review incremental recaps into Subject understanding.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Implement the preferred connected-concepts representation inside the existing desktop/mobile experience, not a separate Learning profile workspace or prototype comparison switcher.
- [ ] Use real account/Subject-scoped approved material and conversation/observation records rather than hard-coded three-node curricula, synthetic history or a two-Subject restriction.
- [ ] Clearly distinguish tentative context, reviewed observations and unexplored concepts. Unexplored means unknown, not weak; links denote related ideas, not proven ability.
- [ ] Do not encode mastery through percentages, ranked node sizes or a hidden ability scale. Material availability alone never becomes evidence of understanding.
- [ ] Selecting a concept exposes understandable observation/context details, qualification and provenance, with a route to the existing conversation when available. Ordinary conversation continuation retains identity.
- [ ] Display newer unreviewed activity separately from the reviewed checkpoint, without promoting tentative text into a conclusion that guides future practice.
- [ ] Keep previously reviewed understanding visible when no current approved sources remain. Empty states explain the difference between missing sources and absence of reviewed observations.
- [ ] Keep AI-derived representation work within initiated workflows, bounded requests and the agreed context policy. Do not introduce a graph engine, scheduled analysis or automatic whole-history reprocessing.
- [ ] Public-boundary tests cover map/detail provenance, reviewed/tentative/unexplored distinctions, source/conversation navigation, Subject/account isolation, persistence, keyboard use and narrow-phone layout without asserting graph implementation details.
