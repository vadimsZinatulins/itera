# Learning Companion

A companion for practising and retaining learning, alongside a personal reference collection.

## Language

**Personal profile**:
Learner-owned identity and context: a picture, name, and optional bio describing interests, background, or goals. Relevant bio context may personalise conversations with the learner’s permission; it is not study material or evidence of understanding.

**Reference note**:
User-owned material kept for lookup and reuse, not as input for learning activities or assessments. AI may read it and assist with cleanup when explicitly invited by the user, but must not modify it autonomously.

**Learning Document**:
User-owned material capturing something the learner is studying within a Subject. Only its approved Practice version may inform learning activities; AI revisions require the learner’s approval.

**Markdown block**:
An individually editable portion of a Learning Document, read as rendered Markdown. Blocks have an order and together form the document’s complete Markdown content.

**Subject**:
An area of study grouping its Learning Documents, learning trail, and practice. Reference notes are shared rather than belonging to a Subject.
_Avoid_: Notebook (for this grouping)

**Draft**:
A working Learning Document, or a revision of an approved one, excluded from learning activities and learning-profile interpretation. Saving a Draft is distinct from approving it for practice.

**Practice version**:
The learner-approved version of a Learning Document available for learning activities within its Subject. It remains unchanged while a Draft revision is being written and represents material encountered, not demonstrated understanding.
